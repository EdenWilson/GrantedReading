Maya rehearsed her violin relentlessly throughout the sweltering afternoons in the cramped apartment situated above the neighborhood bakery, and her instructor cautioned repeatedly that the forthcoming audition would prove ferociously competitive. On the morning of the recital her hands trembled so violently that she could scarcely maintain her grip on the bow. She persevered through the composition regardless, misplaying three notes near the conclusion. The adjudicators declined to select her for the youth orchestra.
