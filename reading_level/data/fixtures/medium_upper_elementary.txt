Every spring, the students in Room 12 plant a garden behind the school. They begin by testing the soil to see whether it holds enough water. Last year the tomatoes struggled because the ground stayed too dry through July. This time the class added compost from the cafeteria, which helps the soil trap moisture. Each student is responsible for one row of vegetables. They keep a notebook and record how tall the plants grow each week. When the harvest comes, the class donates most of the food to a shelter downtown. The rest is cooked in the school kitchen for a shared lunch. Mr. Alvarez says the garden teaches patience better than any lesson he could write on the board.
