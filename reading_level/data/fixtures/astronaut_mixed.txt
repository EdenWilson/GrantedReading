Mia had always harbored an ardent aspiration to become an astronaut, and she pursued that ambition with a tenacity her teachers found remarkable. The preparation was arduous, because the curriculum demanded a meticulous comprehension of orbital mechanics and she often studied until the small hours of the morning. On melancholic evenings she would gaze at the luminous constellations and contemplate the sheer magnitude of the cosmos. The training facility was a cacophony of clattering machinery, and the perpetual commotion made concentration exceedingly difficult for everyone. Her instructors were adamant that only the most resilient candidates would endure the rigorous evaluation ahead of them. Mia was determined to demonstrate her competence, so she rehearsed every procedure until it became instinctive. When the final assessment concluded, the administrators commended her extraordinary perseverance and offered her a coveted position on the forthcoming expedition.
